# Conversion strategy – Insinööritoimisto KJ Oy

## Business model (verified vs. assumed)

- **Verified:** sells engineering services (kuntotarkastus, kuntotutkimus, kosteusmittaukset, vastaavan työnjohtajan
  tehtävät, valvonta) and accredited concrete testing (FINAS T359) to both businesses and private persons in Joensuu,
  Pohjois-Karjala and Eastern Finland. Fixed published prices for most services; VTJ/valvonta priced case by case.
  Work starts from contact by phone or email; one named contact person covers all areas.
- **Assumption:** inspections and measurements bring frequent consumer jobs; the lab and VTJ bring recurring B2B work
  (contractors, precast/ready-mix producers, builders). Revenue split is unknown and not claimed.

## Customer segments

| Segment | Need / pain | Decision criteria | Path on site |
|---|---|---|---|
| House sellers, buyers, realtors | Reliable condition info before a deal; time pressure | Impartiality, method (KH 90-00394), price, availability (weekends) | Home → Kuntotarkastus → calculator → form/phone |
| Homeowners with a suspected damage | Is it moisture/mould? How big? | Credibility, what is opened/sampled, price range | Section drawing → Kuntotutkimus / Kosteusmittaus |
| Self-builders, small builders | Permit requires a responsible site supervisor | Qualification, scope, price agreement | Paths → Vastaava työnjohtaja → form (project description) |
| Contractors, precast & ready-mix, consultants | Compressive strength results fast and valid | Accreditation scope, turnaround, price, ordering ease | Lab band → Laboratorio → online order / PDF |
| Flooring contractors | Can the slab be coated? | RT 14-10984 method, price | Kosteusmittaukset (betonin kosteus) |

Visitor stages: researching (service pages, FAQ), comparing (prices, accreditation, method, weekend service),
ready (form/phone), returning B2B customers (Laboratorio, order form, Hinnasto, billing details).

## Goals

- **Primary conversion:** a qualified contact – quote request/order through the form, or a phone call.
- **Secondary:** price list and calculator use, PDF order form download, email click, directions/map, service-page visits.

## Customer journey

1. Attention – H1 + authentic lab photo + logo drawing; offer and location understood in seconds.
2. Value – fixed prices, FINAS T359, weekend service, travel included for inspections.
3. Relevance – two paths and the interactive section drawing map problems to services.
4. Trust – person card, registry facts, accreditation scope link, partners, real photos.
5. Objections – FAQ, comparison (tarkastus vs. tutkimus), VAT views, what is/isn't included.
6. Action – service-specific CTAs pre-select the form; phone always one tap away.

## Conversion barriers addressed

Missing CTAs and form; prices separated from services and confusing VAT bases; lab ordering only by PDF; consumer and
B2B content mixed; contact person hidden; mobile without quick contact; stale information.

## Hypotheses (to be measured, not results)

1. Showing the inspection price next to a build-year input increases inspection requests from price-checking visitors.
2. An online lab order mirroring the official form lowers friction for repeat B2B customers vs. PDF only.
3. Service-specific pre-selected forms produce more complete, better-qualified requests.
4. The mobile call bar increases phone contacts from mobile visitors.
5. Separate service pages with location-specific titles improve visibility for "kuntotarkastus Joensuu"-type searches.

## Measurement

See CONVERSION_MEASUREMENT.md. Primary metric: qualified requests (form submissions + calls) per month, segmented by
service. Do not treat clicks as sales.
